01
First discipline

Workplace
Strategy.

We create modern and conscious work environments that support both employees and the organisation's business goals. We look at the office beyond square metres - we combine data analysis with designing spaces that support wellbeing, attract international talent, and enable innovation.

Methodology

We don't start od ankiety.

Unlike the traditional approach, we don't ask people what they want. Why? Because expectations are always shaped by limitations - past experiences, current frustrations, or a lack of exposure to new possibilities.

→ observation before the question
Employee Experience

We listen to how people work.

We take time to understand the diverse perspectives of stakeholders. We uncover how people really work, how they collaborate, and how they experience their environment - in interviews and focus groups we build a common vision of the future.

→ shared vision of teams + business
Effects

Strategy based on evidence.

Analysing client data ensures that workplace strategy is not based on guesswork or generic benchmarks but on real evidence. A solid foundation for strategic design decisions.

→ future-ready, flexible, resilient
Methods · five steps

From kick-off
to focus workshops.

Every strategy project begins with the same sequence - five research methods that deliver measurable input to the strategy. From kick-off, through data analysis and onsite visits, to 1:1 interviews and focus groups.
01

Kick-off meeting

With the project group. Project goals, schedule, organisational specifics, ways of working, potential threats.

Online · 1.5 hr
02

Data & documents

Analysis of organisational structure, floor plans, headcount data, building entrance data.

Desk research
03

Onsite visit

Visit to the current office. Analysis of how the space really functions - not how it was supposed to.

Onsite · 3 hr
04

Strategic interviews

5 individual interviews with key stakeholders and directors. Vision, readiness for change, risks.

Online · 1 hr × 5
05

Focus group workshops

3 facilitated workshops with team representatives. The role of the office today, future functions, spatial support.

Onsite · 12 people · 2 hr

02
Second discipline

Project Management
& Technical Advisory.

The role of Project Manager and Technical Advisor. We support the project alongside the client's team and other stakeholders. Structured delivery, technical verification, cost control and risk management across both considered scenarios: STAY or GO.

01

Phase 1.
Decision Support.

STAY or GO? The first project phase is a strategic decision. We coordinate all inputs (design, workplace, optionally McKinsey) so the decision is made on verified data.

  • Coordination of all inputs design, workplace, McKinsey teams
  • High-level CAPEX comparison STAY vs GO for the business case
  • Technical risk identification and comparison of location standards
  • Input to the business case and negotiation strategy
STAY · Phase 2A
Renegotiation + optimisation

Stay and optimise.

We renegotiate the lease, define landlord vs tenant scope, develop the office technical standard. Delivery in an occupied space - in phases, with minimal disruption to operations.

Negotiations & Design

Lease renegotiation

  • Support negotiations with technical arguments
  • Verification of building condition, service charge
  • Definition of landlord vs tenant scope
  • Office technical standard
  • Coordination of IT, Security, AV
Construction Supervision

Phased delivery

  • Project management for phase 1 + phase 2
  • Landlord/tenant coordination
  • Risk and quality management
  • Snagging + handover for each phase
GO · Phase 2B
New office · full delivery

Go and build anew.

New location. Lease negotiations, development of a full office technical standard, design coordination with technical requirements. Full fit-out and end-to-end relocation.

Negotiations & Design

Lease + technical standard

  • Support for lease negotiations
  • Building assessment, fit-out specifications
  • Landlord contribution requirements
  • Full office technical standard
  • Lab / Silent Room replication coordination
Project Delivery

End-to-end delivery

  • Full project management from mobilisation
  • Contractor coordination + supervision
  • Cost & schedule tracking
  • Tenant scope (IT, AV, furniture, lab)
  • Full relocation + snagging + handover
03
Third discipline

Change
Management.

We make sure the organisation moves through office change smoothly, with zero unnecessary disruption. A methodology based on change management models, adapted to workplace transformation - focused on both processes and employee experience.

The employee experience
curve.

From uncertainty through engagement to confident adoption. With clear communication, involvement and support - employees move from resistance, through understanding, to successfully integrating new ways of working into daily routine.
01 · Awareness

Awareness of change

The employee learns about the change. First emotions: uncertainty, curiosity, worry about their place.

02 · Resistance

Resistance

Confrontation with discomfort. Without change management support - the point where many projects collapse.

03 · Exploration

Exploration

The employee starts testing new ways of working. Curiosity arrives, small successes.

04 · Adoption

Adoption

New ways of working become daily routine. Higher engagement and improved productivity.

Stage 01 · Foundation
01
Change Management Strategy
The foundation for all subsequent stages. We identify stakeholders, analyse risks, define KPIs.
Stage 01 · Foundation

Change Management Strategy

  • Kick-off meeting with the project group - 2 hr online
  • Analysis of existing documentation - desk research
  • Workshop with the project group - 5 hr onsite, stakeholder mapping, change impact, KPIs
  • 5 in-depth interviews with key stakeholders
  • Deliverable: Change Management Strategy as a presentation - vision, roadmap, RACI, KPIs, stakeholder analysis
Stage 02 · Project Group

Project group support

  • 3 advisory sessions (3×1 hr online): group structure → action planning → ways of working
  • Training "New office - how to manage this change effectively?" - 3 hr, online or onsite
  • Deliverable: roadmap, RACI, list of risks and recommendations, training summary
Stage 03 · Communication

Communication plan

  • Concept workshop with the communication team - 3 hr onsite
  • Two consultation sessions - 2×1.5 hr online
  • Defining communication principles, plan structure, change narrative, campaign slogans
  • Deliverable: communication brief, communication guide, high-level plan, key messages, educational material
Stage 04 · Leaders

Work with leaders

  • Webinar for managers "New office - what will change and how to make the most of its possibilities?" - 90 min (60 content + 30 Q&A), led by 2 experts
  • Training options: "Manager as a change leader" · "How to organise team work in the office" - 3 hr
  • Deliverable: post-webinar materials, a practical toolkit for managers, key insights
Stage 05 · Ambassadors

Change Ambassadors

  • Two consultation sessions with the project group - 2×1.5 hr online, Ambassador role, selection criteria
  • Ambassador selection support - 3 hr online collaboration
  • Onsite training with workshop elements: "The role of a Change Ambassador in the New Office project" - 3 hr
  • Deliverable: Ambassador process + summary materials + recommendations for next steps
Stage 06 · Team Workshops

Team workshops

  • Workshop "Team rhythm and ways of working" - 4 hr onsite. How and when we work together in the office. Team agreements for hybrid work.
  • Workshop "The new office as team workspace" - 4 hr onsite. Functions of different zones and how to use them.
  • Deliverable: summary materials, team agreements, hybrid work patterns, recommendations
Stage 07 · Monitoring

Monitoring & Evaluation

  • 3 pulse checks - cyclical surveys among all employees
  • 3 meetings with managers - 2 hr each, qualitative insights
  • Consultation package - 5 hr collaboration with the project group
  • Post-Occupancy Evaluation (POE) - survey 3 months after the move
  • Workplace Data Analysis - desk usage, meeting rooms, peak days
  • Deliverable: regular monitoring reports + comprehensive evaluation report
